## Summary
Answering a student (Rohan) who felt he had nothing unique to write about beyond coding, Khalil argues the medium doesn't matter — what matters is giving back or enriching time. Low-effort content (e.g. generic React to-do guides) has lost value to ChatGPT; differentiation now comes from authentic experience: pick a non-trivial problem or curiosity, chart the path, and teach what you learned. Uniqueness comes from intertwining content with your life experience, spanning beyond coding into a personal brand. Writing also signals your values and helps you find aligned companies; code is just a medium, and solving problems is always the valuable thing.

## Core ideas
- Course philosophy: **give back time or enrich time** — applies to software artifacts and writing alike.
- Goal is **not** to find something unique about coding; it's to **solve problems you have and share them**.
- Example: a React to-do list guide has little value now (ChatGPT can produce it). More valuable: "here are the to-do list components/libraries I tried for my project, this one was best, and here's why."
- The internet has discarded low-effort content — "gone are the days of just farming content."
- Required now: **authentic experience** — solve a hard problem, chart new territory, then teach/show what you did and saw.
- Khalil's example: frustrated his code turned to mush after graduating → read many books, coded a ton, wrote along the way; writing oriented around **charting the path** (a higher-level problem), not tool how-tos.
- Recommendation:
  1. Write down problems you want to solve in life or work.
  2. Write down things you're curious about (e.g. building AI apps, making a product that makes money).
  - Pick a non-trivial goal/challenge you're genuinely motivated by — it's a journey.
- Value = charting the territory and turning around to show people — a **hero's journey / call to action**.
- **Content becomes unique when intertwined with your experience of life.**
- Era of the **solopreneur / one-person business**.
- **Value differential**: value shifted for front-end devs due to ChatGPT; same shift in writing/content/audiences — fluff supply high, value of real human experience high.
- Blend multiple life areas into a **personal brand**; don't limit yourself to coding — include passions and curiosities.
- Code is the medium to give back/enrich time; care about code when relevant to solving problems.
- Rohan's profile: mental health, logistics, crypto, overcame extreme poverty — multi-dimensional beyond software developer.
- **Company alignment**: write code somewhere aligned with your values; writing about what you care about markets you, differentiates you, and vets/shows what you're passionate about (e.g. mental-health writer → Headspace).
- Put yourself out there authentically → find the right people and aligned opportunities.
- Naval (attributed): learn to **write, build, and sell** — coding = build; writing; selling = selling who you are, done through writing.
- Be a **value creator**: experience problems, dive in, create unique systems, then share those systems/solutions.

## Named frameworks / mental models
- **Give back / enrich time** — test of value for any artifact or content.
- **Problem-journey content model** — pick problem/curiosity → chart path → teach what you learned.
- **Value differential** — AI shifts where value lies; move to what's scarce (real experience).
- **Write, build, sell** (Naval) — three skills for developers.
- **Personal brand as value creator** — blend life domains; share systems you built to solve your problems.

## Definitions
- **Value differential** — the shift in what's valuable caused by tools like ChatGPT (commoditizing basic how-to content/front-end work).
- **Personal brand** — creating content across your problem areas and passions, positioning yourself as a value creator.

## Rules & heuristics
- Share experience-based comparisons and decisions, not generic tutorials.
- Choose non-trivial problems you're motivated to pursue over time.
- Don't restrict writing to coding topics.
- Seek companies aligned with your values; let your writing signal them.
- Solving problems > coding for coding's sake.

## Anti-patterns / mistakes called out
- Low-effort content farming / generic tutorials easily generated by ChatGPT.
- Searching for a "unique coding angle" instead of solving real problems.
- Coding for coding's sake.
