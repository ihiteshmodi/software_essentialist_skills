## Summary
Brief Q&A clip. A student shares that the book *Essentialism* and working backwards with TDD force you to "kind of have faith," countering the tendency to dilly-dally in life. Khalil says he applies the same backwards process daily: define the next desired state of the universe, then let it carve the steps back to the present. He ties this to the Mastery Foundations content.

## Core ideas
- Working backwards with TDD forces you to have faith (student).
- In life it's easy to dilly-dally and not accomplish what you want (student).
- Khalil's daily practice — write down:
  1. What do we need to have next?
  2. Where do I want to be next?
  3. What is the state of the universe I will be in?
  - Then that end state "carves the pieces moving back to make that happen."
- Not new — previously drawn in the Mastery Foundations content; "It's all come together now."

## Named frameworks / mental models
- **Thinking backwards (daily)** — purpose: turn vague goals into concrete steps. Steps: define desired future state → derive preceding steps. When: daily planning, TDD (assert first), state machines.
